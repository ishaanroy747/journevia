Traveling to Belgium From India? The Complete 2027 Guide for Indians

A practical 2027 guide for Indian travellers covering Belgium's Schengen tourist visa, application preparation and trip planning.

Travel planning guide For Indian travellers 2027 Guide

Belgium is a rewarding destination for Indian travellers who enjoy historic architecture, charming European streets, art, food and easy connections to other Schengen countries. From exploring Brussels' grand squares to walking beside Bruges' canals and discovering Ghent's medieval landmarks, a trip to Belgium can be part of a memorable European holiday.

However, before booking your journey, it is important to understand the visa process. Most Indian passport holders travelling to Belgium for tourism need a Belgium Tourist Visa for Indians, generally issued as a short-stay Schengen visa (Type C). The application involves more than completing a form: travellers must prepare supporting documents, demonstrate sufficient funds, arrange suitable travel medical insurance and attend the required visa appointment.

This 2027 guide explains the visa requirements, application process, estimated costs, processing considerations and travel-planning tips for Indians visiting Belgium.

Do Indians Need a Visa to Visit Belgium?

Yes. Indian ordinary passport holders generally need a Schengen visa before travelling to Belgium for tourism or a short holiday.

Belgium is part of the Schengen Area, which allows eligible travellers to move between participating countries during the authorised period of their visa, subject to its conditions.

For tourism, the relevant category is usually the Belgium short-stay visa (Type C) from India. It is designed for visits of up to 90 days within any 180-day period.

The visa may be issued for a single entry, double entry or multiple entries. The number of entries and permitted duration are decided by the visa authorities; applying for a multiple-entry visa does not guarantee that one will be issued.

If your purpose is to study, work or live in Belgium for more than 90 days, you will generally need to explore the appropriate long-stay visa instead.

What Is the Belgium Schengen Visa for Indian Citizens?

The Belgium Schengen visa for Indian citizens is a short-stay travel authorisation for eligible visits to Belgium and other Schengen countries.

It can be relevant for:

  • Tourism and holidays
  • Visiting family or friends
  • Short business visits
  • Cultural or sporting events
  • Other permitted short-stay purposes

One important point is that you do not automatically have to apply through Belgium simply because it is one of your planned destinations.

You should generally apply through the Schengen country that is your main destination, based on the purpose or length of your stay. If you plan to spend an equal amount of time in multiple Schengen countries and there is no main destination, the first-entry country may determine where you apply.

For example, if your itinerary includes six nights in Belgium and three nights in France, Belgium would normally be the main destination for your application.

Belgium Tourist Visa Requirements for Indians

Preparing a consistent document file is one of the most important parts of the application. The documents should support your travel purpose, financial situation and intention to leave the Schengen Area before your authorised stay ends.

The Belgium tourist visa requirements for Indians generally include the following categories. The final checklist must be checked against the current official requirements for your application category.

Personal and passport documents

  • Valid Indian passport
  • Completed Schengen visa application form
  • Recent passport photographs meeting the required specifications
  • Copies of relevant passport pages
  • Previous passports and visa copies, where applicable

Check the passport validity and blank-page requirements before applying. The passport should generally have at least two blank pages and remain valid for at least three months after the planned departure from the Schengen Area.

Travel and accommodation documents

  • Flight itinerary or reservation
  • Hotel reservations or accommodation proof
  • Day-wise travel itinerary
  • Details of travel between destinations, where relevant
  • Invitation letter and host details if staying with family or friends

Your travel plan should be realistic. The dates, accommodation details and destinations should match the information entered in your application form.

Financial documents

Applicants should provide evidence that they can afford the trip.

Depending on their circumstances, useful documents may include:

  • Recent bank statements
  • Salary slips
  • Income Tax Returns
  • Employment or business income evidence
  • Sponsorship documents, where applicable
  • Evidence of who will pay for the trip

There is no universal bank balance that guarantees approval. The financial evidence should make sense in relation to the length of the visit, accommodation, planned activities and the applicant's personal circumstances.

Travel medical insurance

Travel medical insurance is mandatory for a short-stay Schengen visa. The policy should:

  • Provide at least €30,000 in coverage
  • Be valid throughout the Schengen Area
  • Cover the full intended travel period, including arrival and departure dates
  • Include the required medical emergency and repatriation coverage

Check the policy wording and validity dates carefully before submitting it.

Employment and personal circumstances

Additional supporting documents depend on the applicant's occupation and situation.

  • Salaried applicants: Employment letter, approved leave, salary slips and other relevant employment evidence.
  • Self-employed applicants: Business registration, financial records and evidence of business activity.
  • Students: Enrolment certificate, student identification and supporting documents from the institution.
  • Retired applicants: Pension evidence or other documents explaining their financial support.
  • Sponsored applicants: Sponsor's financial evidence and a suitable explanation of the sponsorship arrangement.

Applicants should also provide relevant evidence of their family, professional and economic circumstances in India.

How to Apply for a Belgium Tourist Visa from India

Understanding how to apply for a Belgium tourist visa from India makes the process easier to organise. The general procedure involves completing the online form, preparing the documents and submitting the application through the designated visa application centre.

Step 1: Confirm the correct visa category

For a regular holiday, check whether a short-stay Schengen Type C visa is appropriate. If your trip involves work, study or a stay exceeding 90 days, review the relevant long-stay category instead.

Step 2: Complete the online application

Applicants living in India are directed to use Belgium's official VisaOnWeb system to complete the application form.

Enter your personal information, travel dates, purpose of visit and other requested details carefully. Review the form before submitting it electronically.

Step 3: Arrange your appointment

Follow the instructions for booking an appointment at the designated Belgium Visa Application Centre in India.

The centre and appointment process should be confirmed through the official Belgian mission and VFS information for your place of residence.

Step 4: Prepare and organise your documents

Arrange your passport, application form, photographs, itinerary, accommodation evidence, financial documents, employment papers and travel insurance.

Keep the documents in the order specified by the current checklist. If a document is not applicable to your circumstances, check whether an explanation or alternative evidence is needed.

Step 5: Attend the appointment and provide biometrics

Applicants are generally required to attend in person for a live photograph and fingerprint registration. Children below 12 years are exempt from fingerprint collection under the applicable rules, although other submission requirements may still apply.

Previous Schengen fingerprint records may be reused if they were registered within the preceding 59 months, subject to the authorities' verification.

Step 6: Pay the applicable fees

Pay the visa handling fee and applicable visa application centre service charges through the authorised payment process.

Keep the payment receipts for your records.

Step 7: Track the application

Use the tracking facility provided for your application. The Belgian Embassy or Consulate is responsible for communicating the final decision.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, so avoid making non-refundable travel commitments solely on the assumption that the visa will be issued.

Belgium Tourist Visa Fees for Indians in 2027

The total application expense usually includes the Schengen visa handling fee, visa application centre service charges and any optional services selected by the applicant.

As a reference point, the official information available in late 2026 lists the standard short-stay Schengen visa handling fee at €90 for adults and €45 for children aged 6–11. Children under six are generally exempt from the visa handling fee. The Indian-rupee amount depends on the applicable exchange rate and official fee schedule.

VFS service charges are additional. Optional services such as courier delivery, SMS updates or premium appointments may also increase the total.

For a 2027 application, check the latest fee schedule immediately before your appointment. Fees can change, and visa handling fees are generally non-refundable, including when an application is refused.

Belgium Tourist Visa Processing Time

Processing time depends on the individual application, the season and whether further examination or documents are required.

The VFS information gives a general decision period of around 15 working days, while some applications may take longer. Under the applicable Schengen procedure, processing can be extended in certain cases, including where additional examination or documents are needed.

To reduce last-minute pressure:

  • Start preparing documents well before your intended travel date.
  • Check appointment availability before finalising your itinerary.
  • Submit a complete and consistent application.
  • Allow extra time during busy travel periods.
  • Monitor your application status through the official tracking process.

Applicants can generally submit a Schengen application up to six months before the planned journey. Do not treat the usual processing estimate as a guaranteed decision date.

How Much Bank Balance Is Required for a Belgium Visa?

There is no single fixed bank balance that applies to every Indian applicant. The authorities assess whether the applicant has sufficient and credible financial means for the planned visit.

Your financial documents should help explain how you will pay for:

  • Accommodation
  • Meals and everyday expenses
  • Local transport
  • Sightseeing and activities
  • Return travel
  • Unexpected expenses

A traveller staying in hotels may have different expenses from someone staying with a host. Similarly, a longer itinerary requires a different financial explanation from a short city break.

Avoid unexplained large deposits immediately before applying. If someone else is sponsoring your journey, include the relevant sponsorship explanation and supporting financial documents.

The aim is to demonstrate that your travel arrangements are financially realistic and supported by genuine evidence.

Best Places to Visit in Belgium During Your Holiday

Once your visa and travel arrangements are in order, Belgium offers several destinations worth considering.

Brussels

Belgium's capital is a natural starting point for many visitors. Explore the Grand Place, admire historic architecture and discover the city's museums, cafés and food culture.

Bruges

Bruges is known for its canals, cobbled streets and medieval atmosphere. It is a popular choice for travellers interested in scenic walks, historic buildings and photography.

Ghent

Ghent combines medieval landmarks with a lively city atmosphere. Visitors can explore Gravensteen Castle, historic streets and riverside areas.

Antwerp

Antwerp is recognised for its architecture, fashion, shopping and diamond district. It can be a good addition for travellers who want a different side of Belgian city life.

A practical 7-day Belgium itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Brussels and settle into your accommodation.
  • Day 2: Explore Brussels' historic centre and major attractions.
  • Day 3: Take a day trip to Bruges.
  • Day 4: Discover Ghent's medieval centre.
  • Day 5: Travel to Antwerp and explore its landmarks.
  • Day 6: Enjoy a relaxed day for museums, food or additional sightseeing.
  • Day 7: Complete your departure arrangements.

This is only a sample itinerary. Adjust it to your flight schedule, accommodation and travel preferences. If you plan to visit other Schengen countries, make sure the itinerary accurately reflects your main destination.

Common Reasons for Belgium Visa Refusal

A visa decision is based on the individual application. Some common issues that may lead to concerns include:

  • Incomplete or inconsistent documentation
  • Insufficient or unclear financial evidence
  • An itinerary that does not match the travel purpose
  • Unclear accommodation or travel arrangements
  • Doubts about the intention to leave the Schengen Area before the visa expires
  • Unverifiable or misleading supporting documents
  • Inadequate travel medical insurance

A previous visa refusal does not automatically mean that a future application will be refused. However, applicants should understand the reasons given in the refusal decision and address relevant issues honestly in a new application.

Never submit forged documents or misleading information. These can seriously affect the application and future travel plans.

Important Travel Tips for Indians Visiting Belgium

A little preparation can make the journey more comfortable.

  • Carry important documents: Keep your passport, visa, accommodation details, insurance and return travel information accessible.
  • Plan intercity travel: Check train routes and schedules if you intend to visit several Belgian cities.
  • Understand local payments: Carry an internationally usable payment card and some suitable backup funds.
  • Check the weather: Pack according to the season and your planned activities.
  • Respect your visa conditions: Follow the permitted stay, entry conditions and validity dates printed on your visa.
  • Keep your itinerary realistic: Avoid planning more destinations than you can comfortably cover.
  • Check current entry procedures: Review official travel and border information before departure.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can Indians travel to other Schengen countries with a Belgium tourist visa?

Generally, yes. A valid Schengen visa issued by Belgium can allow travel to other Schengen countries, provided the visa's validity, number of entries and permitted stay are respected. Your application should be submitted to the country that is your main destination.

2. Is a Belgium tourist visa single-entry or multiple-entry?

It can be issued as a single-entry, double-entry or multiple-entry visa. The decision depends on the application and the assessment made by the visa authorities. Multiple entry is not guaranteed.

3. Can I apply for a Belgium tourist visa six months before travelling?

Schengen applications can generally be lodged up to six months before the intended travel date. Check appointment availability and the latest instructions for applicants in India.

4. Do I need to attend the Belgium visa appointment personally?

Most applicants must attend in person for the required photograph and biometrics. Children under 12 are exempt from fingerprint collection, and certain other exemptions may apply under the relevant rules.

5. Is travel insurance compulsory for a Belgium visit visa?

Yes. Short-stay Schengen applicants must provide travel medical insurance meeting the required coverage, territorial validity and travel-period conditions.

6. Can I work in Belgium on a tourist visa?

No. A short-stay tourist visa does not provide general permission to take up employment in Belgium. Travellers intending to work should check the appropriate work and residence authorisation requirements.

Conclusion

Travelling to Belgium from India becomes easier to plan when you understand the visa category, prepare genuine supporting documents and allow enough time for the application process. From completing the online form to attending biometrics and arranging Schengen-compliant travel insurance, each step deserves careful attention.

Before submitting your Belgium Tourist Visa for Indians application, always verify the latest requirements, fees and appointment instructions through official Belgian and VFS channels. Good preparation gives you a clearer path towards enjoying Belgium's historic cities, culture and European experiences.

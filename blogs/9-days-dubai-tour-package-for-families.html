9 Days Dubai Tour Package for Families: How to Enjoy More Without Rushing

Plan a family-friendly Dubai holiday with famous landmarks, desert adventures, theme parks, Abu Dhabi sightseeing and enough time to relax between activities.

Journevia · Family Travel · Dubai Itinerary

Planning a family holiday becomes easier when everyone has time to enjoy the destination instead of rushing from one attraction to another. Dubai offers a mix of famous landmarks, indoor entertainment, desert adventures, shopping and cultural experiences, making it a versatile choice for families travelling from India.

A 9 Days Dubai Tour Package for Families gives you enough time to explore Dubai’s highlights, take a day trip to Abu Dhabi and still leave room for rest. Instead of squeezing every attraction into a few days, you can spread activities across the trip and make the holiday more comfortable for children and adults alike.

Here is how to plan nine days in Dubai, what experiences to prioritise and how to make the most of a family itinerary.

Why Choose Nine Days for a Dubai Family Holiday?

A shorter holiday can cover some major attractions, but families often need extra time for meals, breaks, shopping and activities that appeal to different age groups.

With nine days, you can enjoy a balanced combination of sightseeing, entertainment and leisure. For example, a morning city tour can be followed by a relaxed evening cruise, while a separate day for a theme park means children do not have to rush through rides before another scheduled activity.

A longer itinerary also creates flexibility. If someone needs a quiet afternoon or the family wants to spend more time at a favourite attraction, you have more room to adjust your plans.

Your 9-Day Dubai Family Itinerary

The following itinerary follows the general structure of Travel Saga Holidays’ family package, which includes Dubai sightseeing, a desert safari, an Abu Dhabi excursion, major attractions and a leisure day. Confirm the latest itinerary and availability before booking.

Day 1: Arrive in Dubai and Settle In

Keep your first day simple. After arriving at Dubai International Airport, transfer to your hotel, complete check-in and allow everyone to recover from the journey.

Depending on your arrival time, you might enjoy dinner near the hotel or take a short evening walk. Families travelling with younger children should avoid scheduling a major attraction immediately after a long flight.

Family tip: Keep snacks, essential medicines, chargers and a change of clothes accessible in your hand luggage.

Day 2: Explore Dubai and Enjoy a Marina Dinner Cruise

Start with a half-day city tour to see some of Dubai’s recognisable sights. Depending on the confirmed itinerary, stops may include Jumeirah Mosque, Jumeirah Beach, a Burj Al Arab photo stop and views of Palm Jumeirah.

In the evening, enjoy a dhow dinner cruise at Dubai Marina. The waterfront views, dinner and onboard entertainment offer a different way to experience the city after a day of sightseeing.

This combination works well because it introduces the family to Dubai without requiring a full day of continuous activity.

Day 3: Experience a Desert Safari

Leave the morning free for breakfast, swimming or relaxation before heading out for an afternoon desert safari.

Depending on the operator and selected activity, the experience may include dune bashing, a camel ride, traditional entertainment and a barbecue dinner at the desert camp.

Parents should check age, height and health-related restrictions before confirming activities for younger children. If anyone in the family is uncomfortable with bumpy rides, ask about alternative arrangements.

Family tip: Carry water, sunscreen and light layers for the evening. Follow the activity provider’s safety instructions throughout the excursion.

Day 4: Take a Day Trip to Abu Dhabi

Use the fourth day to explore Abu Dhabi, the UAE’s capital. A full-day city tour can introduce your family to a different side of the country.

Highlights may include Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Corniche, Heritage Village and selected photo stops. The exact attractions depend on the confirmed tour arrangements.

Dress respectfully when visiting religious sites and check the current visitor guidelines before departure. The journey between Dubai and Abu Dhabi takes time, so keep snacks and water handy and avoid adding too many extra activities to the same day.

Day 5: Visit Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall

Dedicate a day to Downtown Dubai. Visiting Burj Khalifa gives the family an opportunity to see the city from one of its most famous landmarks.

Afterward, explore Dubai Mall, enjoy a meal and make time for the Dubai Fountain Show if it is operating as scheduled during your visit.

You do not need to cover the entire mall in one visit. Choose activities that genuinely interest your family and leave time to sit down, eat and enjoy the surroundings.

Family tip: Book timed attraction tickets in advance where appropriate and check the entry time, transfer arrangements and ticket conditions.

Day 6: Discover Dubai Frame and the Museum of the Future

Today combines city views with modern design and interactive experiences.

Dubai Frame offers panoramic views that help visitors appreciate the contrast between older neighbourhoods and the modern skyline. The Museum of the Future provides a different experience, with exhibitions focused on future technologies and ideas.

Check age suitability, ticket availability and entry requirements before finalising your schedule. If your children prefer hands-on activities, discuss the exhibits beforehand so you can decide whether the visit suits their interests.

Day 7: Enjoy a Full Day at IMG Worlds of Adventure

Set aside a full day for IMG Worlds of Adventure, an indoor theme park with themed zones, rides and attractions.

This is an opportunity for children to enjoy a more activity-focused day rather than another round of sightseeing. Families can plan rides around each child’s age, interests and height restrictions.

Arrive with a rough plan, but avoid trying to experience every attraction. Allow time for food, rest and queues, particularly during busy holiday periods.

Day 8: Keep the Day Flexible

One of the biggest advantages of a nine-day itinerary is having a day without a fixed sightseeing schedule.

Use it for shopping, relaxing at the hotel, exploring a neighbourhood or adding an optional experience. Depending on the season and availability, families might consider Global Village, Miracle Garden, a water park or another attraction that matches their interests.

Not every family needs another packed day. If the children are tired, a relaxed breakfast, swimming and an easy dinner can be just as valuable as another excursion.

Day 9: Breakfast, Check-Out and Departure

Keep the final morning straightforward. Have breakfast, check the room for personal belongings and complete check-out before your scheduled airport transfer.

Allow sufficient time for the journey to the airport and airline check-in procedures. If your flight is late in the day, confirm whether a late check-out or luggage storage is available rather than assuming it is included.

Planning a Dubai Tour Package from India

Choosing a Dubai Tour Package from India involves more than comparing the number of attractions or hotel stars. Flight timings, luggage allowances, airport transfers and the ages of children can all affect how comfortable your holiday feels.

Before confirming your plans, consider these practical points:

  • Choose flights carefully: A convenient arrival time can make the first day less tiring.
  • Match the hotel to your needs: Look at room capacity, breakfast, location and access to transport.
  • Check the transfer arrangement: Private airport transfers and shared sightseeing transfers are different services.
  • Plan around children’s energy levels: Avoid stacking long excursions on consecutive days without a break.
  • Review attraction rules: Check ticket times, age restrictions and height requirements before travelling.
  • Keep important documents accessible: Carry passports, visa documents, booking confirmations and insurance details.

Dubai’s cooler months, generally November through March, are popular for outdoor sightseeing. September and October can also suit some travellers, although temperatures may remain warm. Check the forecast and plan outdoor activities around the conditions during your dates.

How to Make the Trip More Comfortable for Children

A successful family holiday is not about visiting the maximum number of attractions. It is about giving everyone a chance to enjoy the experiences.

Keep a small day bag with water, snacks, sunscreen, tissues and any essentials your children need. Schedule breaks after long tours, and let children know what to expect before visiting large attractions or religious sites.

If you are travelling with toddlers, check whether strollers are practical for each planned activity. For older children, let them help choose one or two attractions. Giving everyone a say can make the holiday feel more personal.

Most importantly, leave some unscheduled time. A relaxed afternoon at the hotel or an unplanned stop for ice cream can become a favourite family memory.

Final Thoughts

A 9 Days Dubai Tour Package for Families gives you time to enjoy Dubai’s major landmarks, explore Abu Dhabi, experience a desert safari and spend a full day at a theme park without filling every hour with activities.

The secret to a comfortable trip is balance: plan the major experiences, allow enough time for transfers and meals, and protect at least one day for rest or spontaneous plans.
